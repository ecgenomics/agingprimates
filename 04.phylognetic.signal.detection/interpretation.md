# Interpretation

The longevity quotient (LQ) showed significant phylogenetic signal according
to both statistics considered (Blomberg's K = 0.146, P = 0.0003; Pagel's
lambda = 0.786, P < 0.001). However, the low K value indicates that similarity
among related species is substantially weaker than expected under a Brownian
motion model. This result is consistent with the greater support previously
obtained for an Ornstein–Uhlenbeck process: phylogenetic history contributes to
the distribution of LQ, but its evolution also appears to be influenced by
attraction towards one or more adaptive optima, thereby limiting the Brownian
accumulation of differences through time.
